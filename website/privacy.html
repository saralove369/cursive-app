Privacy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

What we collect

When you request an invitation, we collect the email address you provide. If you use the Codexia & Ink app, it may also store the profile details, writing sessions, and preferences you choose to save.

How we use it

We use this information to operate Codexia & Ink, send the occasional invitation or update you requested, and improve the experience. We do not sell personal information.

How it is kept

We use reputable service providers to host the website and process the information needed to provide the service. We retain it only for as long as necessary for those purposes or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your information, or unsubscribe from email at any time. Contact privacy@codexia.ink.

Questions

For any privacy question, write to privacy@codexia.ink.
